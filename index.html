Simple Bohr model

The dots show a simplified school-level model: protons and neutrons are in the nucleus, while electrons are arranged in shells. The drawing is not to scale.

School-level note: Valency can vary for some elements. Atomicity means the number of atoms in one molecule of an element. For metals and some solid elements, this chart uses the simple school convention of 1. Phosphorus is shown as P₄, sulfur as S₈, and hydrogen, nitrogen, oxygen and chlorine as diatomic molecules.
